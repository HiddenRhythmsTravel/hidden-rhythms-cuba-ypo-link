EO Columbus Chapter Retreat

The Hidden Rhythms

of Colombia

Medellín & Bogotá | January 27 – February 2, 2027

Colombia is a land defined by dramatic transformations, vibrant cultural pulses, and architectural wonders. This January, join fellow EO Columbus members on a bespoke, high-touch journey through the country’s two most compelling capitals: the spring-like valley of Medellín and the high-altitude cosmopolitan epicenter of Bogotá.

  • ✦

    Medellín Transformation Keynote

    An off-the-record briefing detailing the city's historic social and economic evolution.

  • ✦

    Andean Helicopter Curation

    Private flight over the spectacular waters and monoliths of Guatapé and El Peñol.

  • ✦

    Comuna 13 Art & Rhythm

    Immersive street art curation and a private session with the *Son Batá* collective.

  • ✦

    State of the Nation Briefing

    An exclusive political and economic forecast in Bogotá with a leading national analyst.
